## 2. Key findings

1. **Late delivery is the clearest driver of bad reviews.** Late orders average 2.27 stars vs 4.29 for on-time orders; 62% of late orders get a 1-2 star review vs 9% of on-time orders.
2. **Late orders punch above their weight:** they are only 6.7% of deliveries but account for 32% of all 1-2 star reviews.
3. **Revenue is concentrated geographically:** SP alone brings 38% of revenue; the top 3 states bring 63%. Highest late rates (states with meaningful volume): AL (21.4%), MA (17.4%), SE (15.2%).

## 3. Business recommendations


**1. Make on-time delivery the top operating KPI.** Late orders cost 2.0 review points. Set a target to cut the 6.7% late rate, track it weekly by seller and carrier lane, and alert before a package passes its promised date.

**2. Recalibrate delivery promises by region.** Show realistic dates for the slowest destinations (AL (24.5 days), PA (23.8 days), MA (21.6 days)). A slightly longer but honest promise is cheaper than a 1-star review - 'late' is measured against the promise, not against speed.

**3. Launch a seller scorecard with consequences.** Rank sellers on late rate and review score; coach or restrict the 29 sellers averaging under 3.5 stars (R$329,629 of revenue) and reward top-quartile sellers with search visibility. Review the top-decile sellers monthly because they carry 68% of revenue.

**4. Shorten the cross-state lanes.** Cross-state orders are late 8.0% of the time vs 4.5% for same-state orders (avg 15.1 vs 7.9 days). Recruit sellers or add fulfilment/cross-dock capacity near high-demand, high-lateness states; review freight where it is heaviest (MA (26.3% of revenue), SE (24.2% of revenue), PI (24.1% of revenue)).

**5. Protect revenue categories and fix low-rated ones.** The top 5 categories drive 40% of revenue - protect their stock and delivery performance. For the weakest-rated categories (office_furniture, audio, home_confort) audit product-quality and description issues, not only logistics.

**6. Recover unhappy customers and plan for peak demand.** Only 3.00% of customers return and a bad first order lowers that further (2.84% vs 3.01%). Trigger an apology/voucher flow for late orders and low reviews. Peak month was 2017-11 (R$1,003,862); add seller and carrier capacity before peak periods, and reduce the 1.2% cancellation/unavailable leakage with stock-sync checks.

- Trend charts use Jan 2017 - Aug 2018 only; the first and last months of the dataset are incomplete.
- Currency is Brazilian Real (R$); the dataset is a historical sample (2016-2018).
